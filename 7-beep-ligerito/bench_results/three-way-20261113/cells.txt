# `reps=` is appended by the driver (CAMPAIGN_REPS).

# Every cell opens the same commitment with Flock's split ladder (fold challenges in GF(2^256), its embedded
# `fast` or `slim` configuration at 128 bits), with the affine ladder and with the BEEP ladder at two slacks,
# and with the timing control of each BEEP ladder (no points per fold round). The affine and BEEP ladders share
# their shapes and rates. The affine fold grinding is the output of flock/scripts/affine_fold_params.py on that
# ladder (affine_certificates/ has the levels files and the outputs): 2^25 fast and 2^22 slim are the ladders
# that Flock registers; at 2^20 the ladder 1,3,5 has no certificate at its last level, and 1,3,6 has one.
#
# A cell with FLOCK_NO_GRIND=1 runs the same provers with no proof of work (their proofs do not verify): it
# gives the prove times without grinding, to which collect.py adds the proofs of work at their expected cost.

#
# One cell per line: a name, then environment words (NAME=value, upper case) and the arguments of
# flock/crates/flock-core/benches/ligerito_three_way.rs (key=value, lower case). `#` starts a comment.
# `reps=` is appended by the driver (CAMPAIGN_REPS). A cell named `<shape>.<process>` is one of several processes
# of the same shape: collect.py files its rows under the shape.
#
# Every shape opens the same commitment with
#   split         Flock's Ligerito at 128 bits (fold challenges in GF(2^256)): its `fast` configuration at rate 1/2
#                 and its `slim` configuration at rate 1/4. Flock ships no configuration at rates 1/8 and 1/16: there
#                 (`l0_rate=3`, `l0_rate=4`) the configuration is Flock's derivation of `slim` at that rate
#                 (`derive_profile_ladder_at_rate`, a local addition to flock-core);
#   affine        the affine ladder at the slack 0.02 of Flock (fold=: the fold grinding per level);
#   affine-small  the affine ladder with, at each level, the least slack of 0.001, 0.0015, 0.002, 0.003, 0.005, 0.01,
#                 0.02 at which every fold round has a certificate (affine_eta=, affine_fold=);
#   afast, afast-small   the ladders of `affine` and `affine-small` with the prover of affine_fast.rs (one pass per
#                 fold round): the same proofs as Flock's prover, which the bench checks;
#   anf, anf-small       the same two ladders with no out-of-domain sample on the first commitment, with that
#                 prover. The fold rounds of the first level then carry its list (2 L/q in place of 2/q): fold_nf=
#                 and affine_fold_nf= are the fold grinding per level, from `beep_params.py --affine-ladder`. This
#                 is not Flock's protocol: it is the change that BEEP-Ligerito makes, applied to the affine ladder;
#   anfref, anfref-small  the same configurations with Flock's prover;
#   beep          the BEEP ladder at the slacks of eta=, the same at every level, with no binding sample on the
#                 first commitment (the points of the first fold round bind it) and with proofs of work against a
#                 threshold;
#   control       each BEEP ladder with no points per fold round (a timing control, not a sound configuration);
#   beepaff       each BEEP ladder with its fold challenges ground for the error of the affine fold where that has a
#                 certificate at the slack of the level (beepaff=<eta>:<bits per level>/..). An accounting
#                 experiment, not a sound configuration: `python3 scripts/beep_params.py --beep-affine-charge ..`
#                 prints the bits, and affine_certificates/<shape>_beep_affine_charge.txt has the outputs;
#   beepb         each BEEP ladder with the binding sample on the first commitment (as Flock's ladders have it);
#   beepref       each `beep` with the reference prover (three passes per fold round): the same proofs;
#   beepold       the binding sample, the reference prover and proofs of work in bits: BEEP-Ligerito as it was in
#                 the runs 20261113b and 20261113c;
#   beepbits      each `beep` with proofs of work in bits (leading zeros), as in the run 20261113d.
# `beep`, `control`, `beepb` and `beepref` grind their fold, claim-batching and consistency-batching squeezes against
# a threshold that is not a power of two (BeepConfig::with_exact_grinding); the other variants grind in bits.
# The affine and BEEP ladders share their shapes and rates. The affine fold grindings are certified per round:
# `python3 scripts/beep_params.py --affine-ladder log_n=.. k0=.. ks=.. rates=.. [slacks=0.02]` prints them, and
# affine_certificates/ has the outputs. At the slack 0.02 they are those of flock/scripts/affine_fold_params.py
# (2^25 words at rate 1/2, 2^22 words at rate 1/4 and 2^17 words are ladders that Flock registers; at 2^20 the
# ladder 1,3,5 has no certificate at its last level, and 1,3,6 has one; at 2^16 and 2^18 words the ladders have two
# levels: with three, the last level has no certificate; at 2^15 words and rate 1/8 the ladder has two levels, 3,7:
# its last level has no certificate at the rates 2^-5 and 2^-6, and the affine fold has none below the slack 0.02).
#
# A cell with FLOCK_NO_GRIND=1 runs the same provers with no proof of work (their proofs do not verify): it
# gives the prove times without grinding, to which collect.py adds the proofs of work at their expected cost.
#
# 2^24 and 2^25 words: each variant has its own process (a BEEP ladder shares its process with its controls), so
# that no process has part of its memory compressed. The witness and the commitment are deterministic: every
# process opens the same commitment.

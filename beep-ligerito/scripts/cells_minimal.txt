# The cells of the minimal Experiments section of the BEEP paper (research, unaudited).
# [AI-drafted (claude-fable-5-2), 2026-11-16, at the author's request ("a comparison of runtimes and proof sizes of
#  Ligerito and BEEP-Ligerito for n=2^15 to 2^23, at 100 bits of security over a field of size GF(2^128) [...] rate
#  1/2", and "the same as above, at 128 bits of security"); please review]
#
# Format and driver: as scripts/cells.txt (scripts/run_cells.zsh <label> scripts/cells_minimal.txt). A cell
# `m<log_n>-<bits>` runs with the proofs of work, and `m<log_n>-<bits>-nopow` runs the same provers without them.
#
# Instances: messages of 2^15 to 2^23 words of GF(2^128), with a first level of rate 1/2. These are the sizes
# m = 22 to 30 for which Flock ships the profiles `fast` (128 bits) and `fast100` (100 bits).
#
# Variants:
#   split   Flock's Ligerito as it ships (`fast`, or `fast100` at 100 bits): fold challenges in GF(2^256), on
#           Flock's own ladder (rates 1/2, 1/4, 1/8, ..., one more level than the ladder below for some sizes);
#   mcanf   "Ligerito over GF(2^128)" (100 bits only), the baseline of the paper's table: the ladder below with no
#           out-of-domain points in its fold rounds, slack 0.02 as Flock, and every fold challenge ground for
#           Flock's MCA bound of its round over GF(2^128) (beep::mca_ladder_no_first_sample_at; the row union
#           halves from one round of a level to the next). Like `beep`, it has no binding sample on the first
#           commitment, so the two differ in their fold rounds only;
#   mca     the same with the binding sample that Flock's ladders draw on the first commitment
#           (beep::mca_ladder_at): what that sample costs;
#   beep    BEEP-Ligerito at the slacks of eta= (no binding sample on the first commitment, proofs of work
#           against a threshold), every challenge in GF(2^128).
# [AI-revised (claude-fable-5-2), 2026-11-16: `mcanf` added to the cells at 100 bits (the runs `-battery` and `-ac`
#  of that day were measured before, with `mca` as the baseline). The cells did not change after that: in the run
#  `-ac2` the two baselines ground every fold round of a level for its first round, and in the run `-ac3`, the run
#  of record, for its own round (a change of the library, not of the cells)]
# The ladder of `mca`, `mcanf` and `beep`: Flock's rows per level for the size (2^6 rows at the first level, 2^4 for 2^21
# words and 2^5 for 2^22 words, then 2^4), the rates 1/2, 1/8, 1/32, 1/128 (the folded tables stay over GF(2^128),
# so a level has fewer words than Flock's level and, at these rates, a codeword of the same length), and a new
# level as long as the folded table has at least 2^7 words.
